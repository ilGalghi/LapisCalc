<b>LapisCalc</b> is a simple, elegant calculator and unit converter with a Material Design 3 interface. Free, open source, ad-free, and it collects no data.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li><b>Standard calculator</b> with exact arithmetic: large numbers and chained calculations keep full precision.</li>
<li><b>Percentages</b>, including commercial and repeated percentages.</li>
<li><b>Calculation history</b> to review and reuse previous results.</li>
<li><b>Date calculator</b>: difference between two dates, or add/subtract days.</li>
<li><b>Unit converter</b>: angle, time, data, length, area, volume and more.</li>
<li><b>Tip calculator</b>.</li>
<li>Light/dark theme and Material You dynamic colors.</li>
<li>Available in English, Italian, Spanish, French and Romanian.</li>
</ul>

<b>Privacy</b>
No ads, no trackers, no analytics. LapisCalc works fully offline and does not collect any personal data.

Source code: https://github.com/ilGalghi/LapisCalc (GPLv3)
